Part III · What Does Freedom Really Mean?

Source 7 of 14

The Negro in the Present Campaign

In 1892, Frederick Douglass addressed political participation in a periodical read by members of the African Methodist Episcopal community. This source asks students to consider what citizenship means when legal rights exist but their full use and protection remain contested.

Source Context

Political rights become meaningful through participation.

This article appeared in the October 1892 issue of The A.M.E. Church Review . Unlike a speech delivered to a live audience, a periodical article reaches readers through a publication whose editors, readership, and historical moment all shape how the argument is presented.

Use Douglass's argument as evidence, then compare his political concerns with the celebration of voting rights in Source 5.

    Analyze citizenship and participation.

    Compare legal rights with lived reality.
