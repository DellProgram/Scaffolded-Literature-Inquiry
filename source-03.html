The Fugitive's Song

Shift from Douglass describing himself to someone else representing him. This 1845 sheet-music cover presents Douglass as both a fugitive from slavery and a celebrated antislavery figure.

Guiding Questions

Read the image as evidence.

Use both the visual details and the printed words on the cover. Think about what the creator wanted a viewer to believe about Douglass.

  1. 1

    Observe the escape.

    What details in the image show that Frederick Douglass is escaping from slavery? What details suggest where he is trying to go?

  2. 2

    Analyze the portrayal.

    How does the artist portray Douglass in this image? Use words or visual details from the cover to explain whether he is presented as courageous, important, admired, or in another way.

  3. 3

    Connect experience to influence.

    Why might someone in 1845 have chosen to present Douglass as both a fugitive from slavery and a successful abolitionist? How does this image connect his personal experience to his later public influence?
